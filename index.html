Carbon-12 Biological Foundation

Carbon-12 (6 protons, 6 neutrons, 6 electrons) accounts for about 98.93% of natural carbon. Its tetravalent bonding enables single, double, and triple bonds that support macromolecules such as DNA, proteins, and carbohydrates.

  • Atomic number: 6
  • Mass number: 12
  • Valence electrons: 4
  • Role: Backbone of organic chemistry and biochemical energy pathways
