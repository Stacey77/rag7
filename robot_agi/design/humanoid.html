Humanoid Body Studio

A full humanoid assembled from limb chains — torso, head, two arms, two legs — rendered in 3D. Drag to orbit. Runs entirely in your browser.

Pose Spin Zoom

Standalone — each limb is a kinematic chain (the same model as robot_agi.design); the body composes them onto a torso and runs forward kinematics for every joint. The exported URDF is a valid humanoid tree (links + joints with axes and limits).
