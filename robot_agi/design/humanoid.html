Humanoid Body Studio

Describe a body and it assembles — segmented spine, fingered hands, legs or a wheeled base — then renders it in 3D and animates it. Runs entirely in your browser.

Pose Spin Zoom

Standalone — each part is a kinematic node in a tree rooted at the pelvis; forward kinematics runs over every joint each frame. The exported URDF is a valid tree (links + joints with axes, limits, and origins). Nothing is sent anywhere.
