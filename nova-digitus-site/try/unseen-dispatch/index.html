Nova Digitus Designs LLC · Dispatch 001

The machines that ship next, and the ones you haven't seen.

Humanoids left the demo reel and went to work this year — an airport, a car plant, a fulfilment centre. Meanwhile the price of one fell from eighty-five thousand dollars to twenty-five. This deck is the field state: what is actually deployed, what is allocated before it is built, and what has only been seen once, on a stage.

About this dispatch. Every entry is sourced from public reporting and manufacturer disclosure gathered in September 2026. Specifications for unreleased hardware are as announced or reported and routinely change before shipping; prices for pre-release devices are expectations, not quotes. Where a claim rests on a single demonstration, the card says so.

Status is the point. Most coverage of this field flattens everything into "the future is here". It isn't, evenly. A robot working a real shift at Haneda and a robot that talked on a stage at CES are not the same claim, so this deck colours them differently and never blurs the two.
