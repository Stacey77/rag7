Every duty on the label.

The twist

A supplier network that shows its arithmetic.

Sellvia and the platforms like it sell access to an anonymous catalog and take their cut in layers you find out about later. ORBIS runs the opposite way: one price, one printed ledger, one named maker per line, and a fee schedule you can read in a sentence.

01 · Sourcing

Six origins, not one warehouse

Each origin is a standing relationship with two to four cooperatives or small workshops. We buy in container lots, clear customs once, and hold inventory in a US 3PL — so the customer's parcel is a domestic parcel.

02 · Pricing

The Origin Passport

Every product page prints the full landed-cost ledger: maker price, tariff line with its HTS basis, ocean and last-mile freight, the maker royalty, and ORBIS margin. Nothing is folded into "handling".

03 · Maker terms

Six percent, paid on sale

Beyond the wholesale price, 6% of every retail sale returns to the originating cooperative as a royalty. It is a line item on the receipt, remitted quarterly, and the reason a maker will hold capacity for you.

04 · Reseller layer

Flat, not stacked

Resellers who sell the ORBIS catalog under their own storefront pay one subscription and one wholesale price. No reserve hold, no referral percentage, no top-up surcharge, no payout fee.
