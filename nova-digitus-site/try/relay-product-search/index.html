Search the world's merchants. See the landed price.

Relay indexes the same product across every merchant that will ship it to you, then adds the freight, the import duty and the tax for your country — because since de minimis ended, the sticker price stopped being the price. We take a commission from the merchant, never from you, and we print which link pays us.

How Relay makes money. When you use one of our links to buy from a merchant, that merchant pays us a commission. It costs you nothing extra and it never changes the price you pay. Every outbound link on this site is a paid link and is marked as one. Ranking is by landed price, not by what pays us most — switch on Operator view to see exactly what each link earns.

Sample data. Product names describe generic categories rather than specific branded models, and merchant names are placeholders. Prices, shipping and commission figures are illustrative. Commission rates and cookie windows shown by network reflect published ranges researched in September 2026 and change frequently — confirm current terms in each network's dashboard.

Landed-cost estimates. Duty and tax figures are rough planning rates by destination, not classifications. Actual duty depends on the item's HTS heading and its origin. The United States suspended its $800 de minimis exemption in 2025, so low-value parcels into the US now carry duty and a formal or informal entry.
