Etz Chaim Atlasעץ חיים

The Kabbalistic Tree of Life, laid out as a working chart. Every sphere and every lettered path is clickable and carries its Hebrew, its planet or sign, its number, and its tarot key. Layer on the pillars, the four worlds, the descent of the Flaming Sword, the seven gates of the Gnostic ascent, the sound families of the twenty-two letters, or a parallel reading drawn from Dogon cosmology. Calculators at the bottom resolve a name, a birth date, or a Hebrew word to a sphere and light it on the tree.

Correspondences follow the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn's attributions (Mathers / Regardie, 777), the standard Kircher arrangement of the tree, and the Sefer Yetzirah's three-mother / seven-double / twelve-simple division of the alphabet. Where traditions disagree — Heh and Tzaddi, Raphael and Michael — the alternative is named in the entry.
Gnostic and Hermetic material is quoted from the Nag Hammadi codices, the Askew Codex (Pistis Sophia), the Berlin Codex (Gospel of Mary), the Corpus Hermeticum, the Hekhalot literature, and 1 and 3 Enoch. Where a ladder is laid over the tree, the tree is the later frame and the placement is marked as interpretive.
Dogon material follows Griaule & Dieterlen as recorded, with the restudy dispute flagged in its own section. The sphere-by-sphere pairing with the Tree is offered as interpretation, not as anyone's received doctrine.
Built as a study instrument. Nothing here predicts events.
