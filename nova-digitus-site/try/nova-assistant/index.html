Connect to NOVA

Enter the access token set on your NOVA server (NOVA_ACCESS_TOKEN). It stays on this device.

How NOVA worked

Every time NOVA or a subagent thinks and acts, the run is recorded: model calls, tools, retries, limits reached, and whether her self-check changed the answer.

What NOVA knows about you

NOVA keeps what you tell her, what you ask her to remember, and the main idea each time she teaches you. She reads this every time you talk. It's stored on your own NOVA server, and you can delete anything.

NOVA's team

NOVA briefs these subagents when a task needs their tools.

Settings

Conversation mode: tap the mic once and just talk. NOVA answers out loud, then listens again. Tap End, say "goodbye", or stay quiet for 30 seconds to finish. If your NOVA server has a natural voice switched on, she uses it; otherwise she uses the phone's built-in voice.

To install on iPhone or iPad: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
