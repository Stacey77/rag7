All six modules run in this one page. Prices follow a seeded random walk with a shared market factor, so correlations and risk numbers behave realistically, but they are not real quotes. Indicators (RSI 14, MACD 12/26/9, Bollinger 20/2, ATR 14, SMA 20/50) and risk figures (historical VaR, volatility, Sharpe at a 4% risk-free rate, max drawdown) are computed from that data with standard formulas. Holdings are saved in this browser only.
